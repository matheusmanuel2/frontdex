**Finding a good front-end resource takes more time than it should.** Lists on GitHub grow until nobody can read them, and the pretty directories live off whoever pays to appear.

FrontDex is small on purpose. Every resource has a number, a category and one line on why it's worth your time. A person writes that line, not a model.

It started in Luanda, in Portuguese, because that's where we are. The index is open, so is the code, and anyone can propose a resource.
